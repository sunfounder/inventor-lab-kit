Video 16: How to Use Logical Operators in Arduino Programs
==========================================================

This lesson explains how logical operators are used to combine conditions in Arduino programs.

* **Logical operators:** Introduces AND (&&), OR (||), and NOT (!).
* **Condition combining:** Shows how multiple conditions are evaluated together.
* **Control logic:** Demonstrates using logical expressions in decision statements.
* **Practical examples:** Applies logical operators in simple sketches.
