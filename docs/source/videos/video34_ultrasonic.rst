Video 34: How to Measure Distance
==========================================================

This lesson explains how ultrasonic sensors measure distance.

* **Trigger and echo:** Explains sending and receiving ultrasonic pulses.
* **Time measurement:** Demonstrates measuring echo duration.
* **Distance calculation:** Shows converting time into distance.
* **Sensor wiring:** Demonstrates connecting the ultrasonic module.
