Video 28: How to Use Seven Segment Displays
==========================================================

This lesson explains how seven-segment displays are driven by Arduino.

* **Segment layout:** Explains how segments form numbers.
* **Pin connections:** Demonstrates wiring segment pins.
* **Digit display:** Shows how numbers are displayed.
* **Control logic:** Demonstrates turning segments on and off.
