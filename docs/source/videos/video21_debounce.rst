Video 21: How to De-Bounce Switches
==========================================================

This lesson explains why switch bouncing occurs and how it is handled in software.

* **Switch bouncing:** Explains unstable signals from mechanical switches.
* **Timing issues:** Demonstrates rapid state changes during presses.
* **Software debouncing:** Shows how delays or timing checks stabilize input.
* **Reliable input:** Demonstrates reading stable button states.
