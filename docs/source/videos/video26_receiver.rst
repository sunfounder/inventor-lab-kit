Video 26: How to Use Infrared Remotes and Receivers
==========================================================

This lesson explains how infrared remotes communicate with Arduino.

* **IR communication:** Explains how IR signals are transmitted and received.
* **Receiver wiring:** Demonstrates connecting an IR receiver module.
* **Signal decoding:** Shows how remote button codes are read.
* **Command handling:** Demonstrates responding to IR commands.
